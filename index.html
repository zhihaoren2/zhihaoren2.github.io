About

I am a researcher in civil and construction engineering, with research interests at the intersection of artificial intelligence, infrastructure management, construction automation, and data-driven decision making.

My work focuses on developing practical and interpretable computational methods for infrastructure condition assessment, failure prediction, and intelligent construction and asset management.

Research Interests

  • AI and machine learning for infrastructure and construction
  • Infrastructure condition assessment and failure prediction
  • Construction automation and intelligent project management
  • Computer vision and data-driven asset management
  • Explainable and probabilistic AI for engineering applications
